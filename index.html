✦ 1. About The Community: Black Rabbit

Black Rabbit is a student-focused technology community that brings together students who are curious about technology, enjoy solving problems, and want to develop practical skills.

Black Rabbit organizes technical events such as Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, hackathons, technical talks, workshops, and interactive learning sessions. These activities encourage students to experiment with new tools, strengthen foundations, and understand how technical concepts translate into real-world architectures.

The community fosters an open and supportive environment regardless of background or initial skill level. Whether beginning your technical journey or already equipped with production experience, Black Rabbit champions curiosity, continuous improvement, responsible experimentation, and true collaboration.

02 Vision & Grand Trials

Objectives & Challenges

From foundational cipher trials to live prototypes presented before the grand jury.

✦

Vision & Objectives

✦

Our vision is to create an active, welcoming, and student-led technology ecosystem where students can move from learning concepts to applying them in practical situations. We want students to feel encouraged to explore new technical fields, test their ideas, work with others, and develop the confidence to solve real problems.

Hackwarts aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical experience by giving participants a focused environment to plan, build, test, and present a project. It introduces students to artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data science, software development, cloud technology, and open innovation.

The event strengthens problem-solving, creativity, time management, and communication through live interaction with peers, mentors, and industry judges.

“An invitation to dare, build, and innovate beyond the mundane world.”
✦

The Three Wizarding Stages

✦

The hackathon unfolds through three rigorous, carefully orchestrated stages designed to test foundational logic, collaborative endurance, and persuasive execution:

  • ✦
    Stage 1 — Dementor’s Trial Logic, debugging, coding & cipher challenges. Teams solve technical riddles and unlock bonus advantages before project construction starts.
  • ✦
    Stage 2 — Wizarding Build Teams develop practical prototypes based on chosen technical tracks. Dedicated industry mentors provide architectural reviews and guidance.
  • ✦
    Stage 3 — Triwizard Finale Selected finalists take the Great Stage to demonstrate working prototypes, answer rigorous questions, and defend their creations before judges.
✦

Event Overview

✦

Hackwarts runs continuously for 24 hours offline, from 10:00 AM on Day 1 till 10:00 AM on Day 2.

The schedule encompasses registration, house allocation, grand opening ceremony, the first-round trial, intensive building sessions, midnight challenges, final code freeze, live project demos, and the prestigious House Cup coronation.

Submission Requirements

✦

Every participating team must deliver the following by the designated code freeze (08:30 AM Day 2):

  • ✦
    Source Code Repository A clean GitHub/GitLab repository link containing commits made during the hackathon.
  • ✦
    README Documentation Clear setup instructions, architecture breakdown, prerequisites, and execution commands.
  • ✦
    Working Demonstration A live working demo or a recorded video fallback showing full functionality.
  • ✦
    Project Summary A concise explanation of the problem solved, tech stack used, and innovations achieved.
Evaluation Rubrics: Innovation • Technical Implementation • Functionality • Relevance • Usability • Presentation & Q&A.
